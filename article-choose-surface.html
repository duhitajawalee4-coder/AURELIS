SURFACE GUIDE

How to Choose the Right Surface for Your Home

Ideas, practical guidance and design thinking from the AURELIS Journal.

Start with function

Ask how much traffic the room receives, whether the surface is exposed to spills or moisture, and how much maintenance you are comfortable doing.

Then consider visual character

Natural stone can bring veining, pores and tonal variation. More controlled materials can provide a consistent background for cabinetry and furniture.

Think about the whole palette

Compare the surface with flooring, cabinetry, wall colour and lighting. Warm stones can pair naturally with timber and brass, while cooler greys can reinforce a contemporary palette.

Finally, compare samples in context

View samples in daylight and artificial light if possible. A material that looks subtle online can become much more expressive in a real room.

AURELIS tip: Start with how the space is used, then choose the material that supports both function and visual character.
